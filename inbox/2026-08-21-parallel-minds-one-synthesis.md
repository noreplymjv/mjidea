# CEO idea — Parallel minds, one synthesis

## Working title (optional)
Three angles, then a fourth that argues

## The idea in one paragraph
A war room that only generates more text is a content farm. A war room that runs parallel minds and then forces a synthesis — cross-comparing outputs into one sharper answer — is a thinking machine. The think-tank proposal (local heavy + API pro + free tier → synthesis layer → verification) is the same instinct as Mjidea’s genius panel: disagreement is fuel, not noise. The product is not the pile of drafts. The product is the sentence that survives contact with rivals.

## Why I care / lived spark
I already run multi-agent setups (H1/H2, councils, audits). The failure mode is always the same: three reports, zero decision. Synthesis is the adult step.

## Who this is for
People building agent teams, readers curious how ideas get hardened, anyone who confuses “more AI” with “better thought.”

## What I refuse to sound like
Model comparison blog. “Best LLM 2026.” Committee minutes.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Parallelism without synthesis is theater. Synthesis without verification is confidence cosplay. Human gate still owns the irreversible.
