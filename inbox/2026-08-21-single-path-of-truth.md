# CEO idea — Single path of truth

## Working title (optional)
One canonical path per job

## The idea in one paragraph
Duplicate paths feel like redundancy. They are usually a disappearing act: two folders for one job, files vanish into the wrong one, both sides swear they did their part. “One canonical path per job” is boring operations advice that becomes philosophy the moment your life is multi-machine, multi-agent, multi-drive. Clarity is not aesthetics. Clarity is where the work goes so it can be found.

## Why I care / lived spark
GetMeBack’s “ONE folder only” note and MJAI’s Single Path of Truth method are the same bruise. I have lost hours to forks of myself.

## Who this is for
People whose projects live on Passport + DATA + home + cloud and wonder why nothing feels finished.

## What I refuse to sound like
KonMari for files. Enterprise “single source of truth” slides. Minimalism influencer.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
One path per job. Symlinks are fine; competing canons are not. Portability requires naming the home.
