# CEO idea — The seed must self-check

## Working title (optional)
Small seed, verified unfold

## The idea in one paragraph
A system that rebuilds itself from a tiny seed is romantic. A system that verifies each step before knocking the next domino is adult. The MJAI methods registry puts it cleanly: the seed holds the method, not the gigabytes; every method has a test; assumption is how you build broken. That is philosophy for portable companies, agent stacks, and personal knowledge bases: intelligence lives in the procedure that proves itself.

## Why I care / lived spark
I have watched “install scripts” that declare victory while nothing works. The scar is specific: GPU “ready” until you check `size_vram`; services “listening” until you check the bind address. Hope is not a test.

## Who this is for
Builders of self-bootstrapping setups, portable hubs, anyone who ships scripts to future-themselves.

## What I refuse to sound like
DevOps manifesto. “Infrastructure as code” buzz. Bootstrapping guru.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
No test, no claim. Seed = method + verify, not cargo. Bulk reconstitutes; intelligence persists.
