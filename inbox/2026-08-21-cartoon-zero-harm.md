# CEO idea — Cartoon violence, zero harm

## Working title (optional)
Symbolic heat, real safety

## The idea in one paragraph
There is a difference between rehearsing harm and discharging emotion through symbols. A cartoon smash, a juice blender, a trash can — these are rituals that let the nervous system finish a sentence without writing it in the world. “Pick someone to vent on — cartoon fun, zero harm” is not a disclaimer; it is the product’s ethical spine. Culture often offers only two scripts for anger: suppress it, or escalate it. Symbolic play is a third script.

## Why I care / lived spark
GetMeBack only works if the slapstick stays slapstick. The moment it becomes realistic revenge porn, the philosophy collapses. The constraint is the insight: limits create the useful kind of release.

## Who this is for
Builders of emotional tools, parents who worry about screens, anyone who has wanted to throw something and chose a pillow instead.

## What I refuse to sound like
A defense of cruelty. A “violence is healthy” hot take. App-store compliance legalese.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Symbol ≠ endorsement. Real people stay untouched. Playfulness is a safety feature, not packaging.
