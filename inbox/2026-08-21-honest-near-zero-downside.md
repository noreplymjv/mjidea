# CEO idea — Near-zero downside is the honest promise

## Working title (optional)
Don’t sell certainty — engineer recoverability

## The idea in one paragraph
“100% successful, zero loss” is a lie that flatters the listener and corrupts the speaker. The usable promise is narrower and stronger: every idea gets a kill-criteria scorecard so the downside is small and reversible — no inventory, digital-first, demand-tested before spend. Near-zero downside is not timid. It is how you stay in the game long enough for one idea to matter.

## Why I care / lived spark
The venture-factory note that admits this correction is rare AI writing: it refuses the fantasy I might have rewarded. Credibility is a product feature for a digital company, not a soft skill.

## Who this is for
Anyone pitching systems (to themselves or others) who is tempted by absolute language.

## What I refuse to sound like
Risk-management consultant. Motivational “progress not perfection.” Crypto-bro “asymmetry” talk.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Say what you can actually deliver. Recoverable failure > theatrical certainty. Kill criteria are kindness to future-you.
