# CEO idea — No lectures, just play

## Working title (optional)
Anger tools that refuse to preach

## The idea in one paragraph
Most digital “mental health” opens with a sermon: name the feeling, journal the feeling, optimize the feeling. GetMeBack’s founding promise was colder and kinder: turn anger into a short, silly physical action on screen — no lectures, no journaling required. Play is not the opposite of seriousness. Sometimes it is the only door that opens when seriousness has locked itself out. Philosophy can learn from that: some truths arrive as motion first, language second.

## Why I care / lived spark
The early product note said it plainly: no lectures. That line survived into the shipped app’s cartoon tone. I want writing that trusts the reader the same way — invite, don’t tutor.

## Who this is for
People allergic to wellness voice, designers of emotional products, writers who over-explain.

## What I refuse to sound like
Therapist-in-an-app. “5 tips to manage anger.” Anti-therapy crusade.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Play can be a serious instrument. Lecturing is often anxiety wearing authority. Leave room for the user (and reader) to finish the thought.
