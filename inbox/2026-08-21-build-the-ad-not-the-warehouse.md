# CEO idea — Build the ad, not the warehouse

## Working title (optional)
Prove the click before the inventory

## The idea in one paragraph
Most failed products die of premature sincerity: you build the whole thing because you believe. Belief is cheap; demand is not. The venture-factory rule — cheap demand test before build ($0 landing page, pre-order, ad smoke test) — is antifragile product thinking dressed as thrift. “Build the ad, not the warehouse, first” is not cynicism. It is respect for your own time and for the people who would otherwise fund a beautiful ghost.

## Why I care / lived spark
I have lived the opposite pattern: polish the machine, then discover nobody wanted the outcome. The painful lesson is that unfinished belief feels like progress. Clicks are rude, and therefore useful.

## Who this is for
Solo founders, digital makers, anyone with more ideas than runway.

## What I refuse to sound like
Growth-hack Twitter. “Fail fast” slogans. A VC memo about burn rate.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Reversible bets beat heroic bets. Demand evidence before spend. Honesty about uncertainty is stronger than fake certainty.
