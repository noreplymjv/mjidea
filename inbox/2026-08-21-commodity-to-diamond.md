# CEO idea — Commodity to diamond

## Working title (optional)
The diamond factor

## The idea in one paragraph
A spice is a bulk SKU until story, origin, and perception make it a diamond — memorable, premium, scalable without becoming fake luxury. The “Global Alchemist” brief for Nature’s Pinch / Aryam Global said it plainly: turn commodities into diamonds. The essay is not “charge more.” It is: invent psychological and cultural value that cost accounting cannot see.

## Why I care / lived spark
Years of war-room prompts for Classes 29/30/3 — lentils, spices, oils — all fighting the same gravity: commodity pricing.

## Who this is for
Founders of “boring” categories; writers who think premium is only packaging gloss.

## What I refuse to sound like
Luxury-bro flex. Colonial romance about “tribal” poverty. Empty heritage adjectives.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Story must be true. Artisans are families, not props. Price justified by rarity, fairness, journey — never by apology or hype.
