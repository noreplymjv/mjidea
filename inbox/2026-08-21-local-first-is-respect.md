# CEO idea — Local-first is a form of respect

## Working title (optional)
No account, no upload, no apology — or: Your anger shouldn’t train their cloud

## The idea in one paragraph
When an app helps you process private emotion — faces of people you are mad at, photos from your gallery — the default should be: it never leaves the device. GetMeBack’s v1 stance (local only, no accounts, no cloud, no analytics) is not a feature bullet. It is a claim about dignity — a character choice, not “sync.” The industry trained us to trade intimacy for convenience. Refusing that trade is a product philosophy worth writing as philosophy, not as privacy policy.

## Why I care / lived spark
I have watched “helpful” products become surveillance with a smile. For anger and faces, cloud sync is not progress — it is a category error. Respect looks like: the photo stays where the feeling is.

## Who this is for
People tired of signing up to feel something, and founders who still think “accounts” are free.

## What I refuse to sound like
A GDPR checklist. A privacy-startup pitch deck. Paranoid prepper cosplay.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Intimate data earns a higher bar than “engagement.” Local-first is the honest default for emotional tools. Growth that requires betrayal is not growth.
