# CEO idea — Perception beats the stopwatch

## Working title (optional)
Make the wait feel different

## The idea in one paragraph
When a problem feels like speed, we spend fortunes on faster trains, faster apps, faster everything. Often the bottleneck is not physics — it is how the wait is framed. Information, ritual, environment, and meaning can outperform expensive engineering. Rory’s archive keeps circling this: reframe before you rebuild; change the High Line, not only the subway timetable.

## Why I care / lived spark
Mjidea’s Genius Panel exists to catch the expensive-logical answer and ask for the cheap psychological one first.

## Who this is for
Builders, marketers, and anyone stuck optimizing a metric that users experience as a mood.

## What I refuse to sound like
A TED talk about “hacking the brain.” A lazy “perception is reality” shrug that excuses lying.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Don’t deceive. Do redesign experience. Psychological value is real; fraud is not alchemy.
