# CEO idea — Catharsis, then breath

## Working title (optional)
Release it. Then breathe.

## The idea in one paragraph
A complete emotional loop is not “vent forever.” It is a short, silly discharge — then a deliberate return to the body. GetMeBack’s product arc (cartoon vent → calm breathing) is a philosophy of anger: honor the heat, refuse to live inside it. Most wellness apps skip the heat and lecture. Most rage toys skip the landing. The interesting work is the hinge between them.

## Why I care / lived spark
Building a vent app made the sequence obvious: fireworks without a landing pad leave you jittery; breathing without admitting you were furious feels fake. The phrase on the home screen — “Release it. Then breathe.” — is the whole thesis in six words.

## Who this is for
People who get angry and hate themselves for it, and people who build products that touch emotion without turning into therapy cosplay.

## What I refuse to sound like
A mindfulness influencer. A “healthy coping strategies” checklist. A gore fantasy.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Catharsis without calm is incomplete. Calm that denies anger is theater. Cartoon > cruelty.
