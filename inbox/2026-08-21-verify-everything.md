# CEO idea — Verify everything (even human words)

## Working title (optional)
There is no automatically trusted content

## The idea in one paragraph
Before AI, humans already lied, propagandized, and erred. After AI, we add a new fork: AI-assisted good data (verified facts, model as tool) versus bad data (fabrication, deepfakes, deliberate disinfo). The mistake is treating either era as auto-safe. Trust is a process, not a birth certificate for a file.

## Why I care / lived spark
Mj’s content-categorization framework from the mjai workstream — Pre-AI / Post-AI / Good vs Bad Data — belongs in public philosophy, not only in internal ops docs.

## Who this is for
Readers drowning in confident sludge; builders who ingest the web into knowledge bases.

## What I refuse to sound like
Moral panic about AI. Nostalgia that “pre-2022 was pure.” Techno-utopia that “models check themselves.”

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Verification is mandatory across eras. Misuse is a human decision. Reject deceptive AI-assisted content; accept verified tool-use.
