# Harvested idea — Sell the story, not the commodity

**Source:** Nature’s Pinch line sheet + promise page; Marketing war room notes on Harish Bhat / Paper Boat (Nadkarni)

## Core claim
Spices and pulses are commodities until narrative + origin + craft make them desirable. Flavour, like trust, is built over generations. “The soil remembers more stories than we do.”

## Why it matters for Mjidea
Philosophy essays about provenance, inheritance of craft, and price as honesty — not apology. Marketing angle stays subordinated to truth-telling voice rules from Nature’s Pinch copy deck.
