# Harvested idea — One-problem Micro SaaS ladder

**Source:** `/media/mj/DATA/wp/mjai/H1 Business Plan/04-H-Micro_SaaS.md`; `/media/mj/DATA/wp/mjai/H1 Business Plan/00-H-MASTER_SUMMARY.md`

## Core claim
Ship tiny tools that solve ONE painful job (review replies, one-line invoices, 30-day social drafts, price watch, FAQ bot). AI builds; subscriptions fund the server. No VC theater.

## Named concepts
ReviewBot · InvoiceWizard · SocialPilot · PriceWatch · FAQBot

## Why it matters for Mjidea
Essay/product crossover: “small software with a sharp job” as antifragile business philosophy. Also a pipeline of future Mjidea case studies once anything ships.
