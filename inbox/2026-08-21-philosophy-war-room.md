# CEO idea — Why philosophy needs a war room

## Working title (optional)
Philosophy that can travel

## The idea in one paragraph
Most philosophy dies in notebooks because it never survives contact with other sharp minds. I want a standing war room — editors, behavioral thinkers, SEO, security, auditors — so a raw idea becomes something the internet can carry without turning it into sludge.

## Why I care / lived spark
I have watched good thoughts get flattened by “content.” I want the opposite: pressure that makes the thought clearer, stranger, more itself.

## Who this is for
People who think for a living, or want to again.

## What I refuse to sound like
A growth-hack newsletter. A LinkedIn carouse. An AI summary of Stoicism.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Clarity over virality. Virality only if the idea stays true.

## Notes / fragments
Rory-style: change the frame, not just the volume. Genius panel must argue.
