# Harvested idea — Psychological value over functional value

**Source:** `/media/mj/DATA/AllProjects/mjai_work/idea_pool/ideas_raw.txt` (RORY_SUTHERLAND lines); reinforced in `/media/mj/DATA/wp/mjai/H1 Rescue/global-alchemist-prompt.txt`

## Core claim
People often buy the feeling a product creates, not the feature sheet. Change perception and you change value without changing cost.

## Why it matters for Mjidea
Genius Panel default move: ask what *feels* true before what *is* efficient. Essays and products should invent psychological upside, not only utility.

## Fragments
- Perception often matters more than “objective” reality in markets.
- Reframing can raise value with near-zero spend.
- Companion tactic: sell the story of the ingredient, not the commodity (Nature’s Pinch / Harish Bhat lens).
