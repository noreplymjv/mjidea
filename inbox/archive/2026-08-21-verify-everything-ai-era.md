# Harvested idea — No automatically trusted content

**Source:** `/media/mj/DATA/AllProjects/documentation/CONTENT_CATEGORIZATION.md`

## Core claim
Split knowledge by era and intent: Pre-AI vs Post-AI; human-only vs AI-assisted; within AI-assisted, Good Data (verified facts, AI as tool) vs Bad Data (fabrication, deepfakes, disinfo). Every era still needs verification — humans lied before models existed.

## Why it matters for Mjidea
Philosophy + SEO/audit: Mjidea should publish as if every claim will be challenged. Anti-sludge rule is ethical, not just stylistic.

## Fragments
- AI is neutral; misuse is a human decision.
- Accept verified AI-assisted content; reject deceptive AI-assisted content.
- Extra scrutiny tools: detection, metadata, deepfake/voice checks — for systems that ingest the web.
