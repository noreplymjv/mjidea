# Harvested idea — Trust isn’t certified. It’s grown.

**Source:** `/home/mj/Documents/natures pinch/html /our-promise-page.md`

## Core claim
Certificates prove a snapshot. Taste, named origins, and a short supply chain prove a relationship. Honesty that needs no stamp is the harder (and more memorable) position.

## Signature lines (voice gold)
- “Trust Isn't Certified. It's Grown.”
- “We don't ask paper to prove what a single pinch can.”
- “What's on the Label Is All That's in It.”
- “Cheap spices are everywhere. This is the other kind.”

## Why it matters for Mjidea
Essay-ready brand philosophy that generalizes beyond spices: proof culture vs lived truth; paperwork theater vs tasting reality.
