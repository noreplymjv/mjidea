# CEO idea — Local-first as character

## Working title (optional)
Your anger shouldn’t train their cloud

## The idea in one paragraph
When a product touches private emotion — a face you vent at, a breath you take after — shipping that data to the cloud is not “sync.” It is a character choice. GetMeBack’s v1 bet was local-only: no accounts, no uploads. The philosophy: convenience that sells your nervous system is not progress.

## Why I care / lived spark
Building a vent app made privacy feel non-negotiable. Cartoon catharsis is play; clouding the target photo is not.

## Who this is for
People choosing apps that touch mood, photos, or health; builders tempted to add accounts “for later.”

## What I refuse to sound like
Paranoid manifesto. Privacy-product marketing brochure. Anti-cloud absolutism for every use case.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
For emotional vent tools, local-first is the default moral stance. “Trust us” is not a feature.
