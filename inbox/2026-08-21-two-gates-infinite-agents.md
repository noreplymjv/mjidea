# CEO idea — Two gates, infinite agents

## Working title (optional)
You touch it twice

## The idea in one paragraph
A digital company should not ask the human for every micro-decision. That is how agents become needy employees. The venture-factory design is sharper: Mj touches the work at two gates only — approve the idea, authorize money/go-live. Everything between (research, viability, build, audit, SEO) can be digital teams arguing in a war room. Delegation without gates is chaos. Gates without delegation is a bottleneck wearing a crown.

## Why I care / lived spark
Mjidea’s auto-run rule and the factory’s two-gate model are the same instinct: protect judgment for irreversible moments; automate the friction of doing.

## Who this is for
Builders of agent systems, CEOs drowning in approvals, people designing “companies” that are mostly software.

## What I refuse to sound like
Corporate RACI charts. “AI will replace managers.” Autopilot utopia.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Spend, publish, and legal claims stay human. Drafting and arguing can be digital. Gate 1 and Gate 2 are not optional ceremony.
