# CEO idea — Perception is the real product

## Working title (optional)
Objective reality is overrated

## The idea in one paragraph
Behavioral economics (the Rory vault I collected) keeps returning to one uncomfortable claim: humans do not buy objective reality — they buy framed perception. Price feels different by the pod than by the packet. Value is colored by context. Tiny asymmetries in wording change what “expensive” means. For Mjidea, that is not a marketing trick to exploit. It is a writing craft: change the game board of how a thought is received, instead of shouting the same thought louder.

## Why I care / lived spark
I scraped and kept hours of counterintuitive advertising talk because it matches how I want philosophy to travel — not as a lecture, but as a reframe people can hand to a friend.

## Who this is for
Writers, marketers who still have a conscience, product people stuck optimizing features while the story is wrong.

## What I refuse to sound like
Manipulation how-to. “Hack the brain” YouTube. Ogilvy cosplay without craft.

## Non-negotiable beliefs (do not dilute)
Reframe ≠ deceive. Perception work that helps someone see clearer is ethical; perception work that traps them is not. Loudness is a lazy substitute for frame.
