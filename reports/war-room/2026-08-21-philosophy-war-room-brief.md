## CEO raw idea

Philosophy needs a standing war room so raw ideas become essays that travel without becoming sludge. Clarity over virality; virality only if truth survives.

## Genius reframes

### Options (3)
1. **Pressure-cooker philosophy** — good ideas deserve adversaries, not applause.
2. **Anti-content content** — build a machine whose output refuses to sound like content.
3. **Behavioral reframe (Rowan)** — people share essays that make them look *discerning*, not loud. Design for status-of-taste, not dopamine.

### Recommended
#3 + #1: frame Mjidea as a private cabinet of sharp minds that publishes only what survives disagreement — sharing signals taste.

### Why it can go viral (without trash)
A single quotable line: “Most philosophy dies of loneliness, not of being wrong.”

### Risks
Sounding elitist. Mitigate with plain language and lived stakes.

## Chosen angle
