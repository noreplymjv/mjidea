## Key findings
1. Free play with other children declined sharply over ~50 years while youth anxiety/depression/helplessness rose — Source: [Gray 2011, American Journal of Play PDF](https://www.museumofplay.org/app/uploads/2022/01/3-4-article-gray-decline-of-play.pdf) — Accessed: 2026-09-02 — Notes: causal argument is correlational but mechanism (autonomy, emotion regulation, peer negotiation) is well specified.
2. Play builds executive function and buffering caregiver relationships; AAP urges defending unstructured play — Source: [AAP Power of Play (2018)](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/142/3/e20182058/38649/The-Power-of-Play-A-Pediatric-Role-in-Enhancing) — Accessed: 2026-09-02.
3. Autonomy/competence/relatedness needs; controlling contexts undermine intrinsic motivation — Source: [Deci & Ryan 2000 PDF](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/2000_DeciRyan_PIWhatWhy.pdf) — Accessed: 2026-09-02.
4. Upper-middle-class / high-achieving youth show elevated maladjustment under achievement pressure — Source: [Luthar et al. 2013 PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4215566/) — Accessed: 2026-09-02.
5. Goodhart: measures collapse as control instruments; popular form “measure → target” — Source: [Goodhart's law overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodhart%27s_law) — Accessed: 2026-09-02.
6. Gardener vs carpenter parenting metaphor — Source: Gopnik (2016) *The Gardener and the Carpenter* — Accessed: 2026-09-02.
