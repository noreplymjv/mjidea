## Core Citations

1. **The 'IKEA Effect': When Labor Leads to Love**
   - Authors: Norton, M. I., Mochon, D., & Ariely, D. (2012)
   - Publication: *Journal of Consumer Psychology*, 22(3), 453-460.
   - Key Insight: Labor leads to love only when labor results in successful completion of tasks.

2. **Costly Signaling Theory and Evolution**
   - Authors: Zahavi, A. (1975); Grafen, A. (1990)
   - Publication: *Journal of Theoretical Biology*, 53(1), 205-214.
   - Key Insight: Reliable signaling requires signals to be costly to fake.

3. **Alchemy: The Surprising Power of Ideas That Don't Make Sense**
   - Author: Rory Sutherland (2019)
   - Publisher: William Morrow / Penguin Random House.
   - Key Insight: Psychological value diverges radically from mathematical efficiency.
