150 minutes is not a personality

Minutes beat identity.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · life · movement · life

← All ideas

Activity guidelines are not a vibe. They are minutes.

WHO reports nearly 1.8 billion adults — about 31% — did not meet recommended physical activity levels in 2022.1

Two 25-minute walks on the calendar like meetings.

Motion is mood chemistry you can schedule.

Miss the myth of perfect; keep the minutes.
