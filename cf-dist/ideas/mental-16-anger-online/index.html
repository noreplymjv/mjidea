When anger online becomes the air you breathe

Anger online is a human problem with a workable door: draft-not-send rule.

2 August 2026 · mental · mental · humanity · solutions · society

← All ideas

Call it a crisis if you want. It behaves more like a bad habit with PR.

anger online thrives because it pays someone — a platform, a habit loop, a workplace norm — even when it costs you.

Students feel it as a performance that never clocks out. The advice is everywhere. The defaults have not moved.

The reframe

Treat attention like a budget with line items, not a vibe.

A door that opens

Do this: draft-not-send rule. Keep the bar low enough that a tired human clears it. Raise the bar only after the ritual sticks.

Humanity’s younger half does not need more content about discipline. They need adults willing to change defaults in homes, schools, and products.

Leave one cleaner default behind you this week. Then another.
