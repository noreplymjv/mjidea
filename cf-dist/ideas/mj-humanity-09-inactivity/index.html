1.8 billion barely move

Chairs became the default habitat.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · humanity · movement · health

← All ideas

We invented machines to spare our bodies, then wondered why the body filed a complaint.

WHO reports nearly 1.8 billion adults — about 31% — did not meet recommended physical activity levels in 2022.1

Movement is not a gym identity. It is minutes.

Attach a 20-minute walk to an existing habit. Miss once, not twice.

Stillness sold as comfort is often deferred disease.
