Belonging is a health system

Neighbours treat reasons to stay alive.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · humanity · loneliness · community

← All ideas

Hospitals treat bodies. Belonging treats the will to return tomorrow.

The WHO Commission on Social Connection estimates about one in six people globally report loneliness, with loneliness associated with on the order of 871,000 deaths a year in the period examined.1

Community is preventive medicine with a doorbell.

Restart one recurring local ritual and show up twice before judging it.

Health systems that ignore belonging refill the same beds.
