# Content War Room (≥5)

Members: Elena Voss, Marcus Hale, Priya Nair, Sam Ortiz, Leah Kim (+ Drew Fontaine for claims).

## Mission

Write a **superhuman human** essay — philosophy/ideas voice. Not corporate. Not AI. Not LinkedIn sludge.

## Process

1. Marcus distills the idea into a thesis + stakes
2. Priya locks narrative arc
3. Elena sets voice constraints from `brand/VOICE.md`
4. Draft (800–1600 words unless brief says otherwise)
5. Sam cuts 15–30%
6. Leah adds one metaphor max (if needed)
7. Drew flags weak claims
8. Elena final humanize pass

## Anti-AI checklist (must all pass)

- [ ] Varied sentence length; some fragments OK
- [ ] One strong opinion that could lose readers
- [ ] Zero banned phrases from VOICE.md
- [ ] No fake certainty; admit unknowns
- [ ] Sounds like a person who has lived, not a summary bot
- [ ] Title is specific, not “X: A Complete Guide”
