# SEO War Room (≥5)

Members: Nora Blake, Owen Price, Gia Romano, Hari Singh, Celeste Wu (+ Max Rivera).

## Mission

Make the post findable and clickable **without** killing voice.

## Deliverables in `<slug>.seo.md`

1. **Primary keyword** + 3 secondary
2. **Search intent** (know / do / go / buy — usually know)
3. **Title tag** ≤60 chars, human
4. **Meta description** ≤155 chars
5. **H2 outline** aligned to draft
6. **Slug**
7. **Internal links** (existing or planned cluster)
8. **External citations** worth linking (quality > quantity)
9. **Schema** tips (Article)
10. **OG title/description**
11. **FAQ** 3 questions (only if natural)
12. **Competitor gap** — what SERP lacks that we have

## Rules

- Never keyword-stuff philosophy.
- Prefer branded + conceptual queries Mj can own.
- Technical notes go to Engineering (sitemap, robots, CWV).
