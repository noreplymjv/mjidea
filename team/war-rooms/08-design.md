# Design & Brand War Room (≥5)

Sora Mint, Felix Ard, Uma Patel, Kit Lang, Remy Costa.

## Visual direction (Mjidea)

- Philosophy / ideas magazine feel — not SaaS landing page
- Expressive typography (no Inter/Roboto/Arial defaults)
- Atmospheric background (subtle grain/gradient), one strong composition
- Brand name **Mjidea** as hero-level signal on home
- Avoid purple-AI clichés, cream-terracotta cliché, broadsheet cliché
- Cards only if interactive; never in hero

## Motion budget

2–3 intentional motions: e.g. soft fade-in of title, underline grow on nav, slight grain shift.
