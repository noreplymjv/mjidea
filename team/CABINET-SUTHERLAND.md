## Operating doctrine

1. Every essay: **current human problem** + **possible solution** (behavioral or practical, not empty hope).
2. Open with tension, not a definition.
3. One **reframe** mid-piece (the Rory move).
4. Close with a residual question or a quiet directive.
5. Ban AI filler (see `brand/VOICE.md`).
6. Batch publish only under CEO “build the library” mandate; otherwise drafts → approve.

## Mission for this build

Ship **500+** essays on problems facing humanity / younger generations, with solutions that can travel socially — insight density, not ragebait.
