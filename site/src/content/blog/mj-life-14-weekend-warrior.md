Binge effort does not cancel a sedentary week.

WHO reports nearly 1.8 billion adults — about 31% — did not meet recommended physical activity levels in 2022.[^1]

Sprinkle movement daily; keep weekends for joy.

Consistency beats confession.

Hero weekends cannot rescue ignored weekdays.
