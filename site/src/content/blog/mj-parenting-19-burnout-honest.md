Parental exhaustion is real even without a viral statistic.

CDC data for 2024 show 30.5% of U.S. adults average less than seven hours of sleep in a 24-hour period.[^1]

Ask for help in one specific sentence this week.

Strong parents are allowed to be tired out loud.

Vague martyrdom helps no one.
