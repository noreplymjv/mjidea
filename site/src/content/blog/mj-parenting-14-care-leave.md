Care keeps millions of women outside paid work — by design of systems, not by accident of love.

ILO estimates that in 2023, 708 million women cited care responsibilities as the reason for being outside the labour market.[^1]

If you employ people, treat flexible care as strategy, not perk theater.

Measure promotions by caregiving status.

Talent lost to unpaid care is a fiction on the books.
