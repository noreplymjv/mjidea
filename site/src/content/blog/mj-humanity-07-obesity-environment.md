If the default aisle rewards ultra-processed convenience, “discipline” is swimming upstream.

WHO reports that in 2022, one in eight people worldwide were living with obesity, and 2.5 billion adults were overweight.[^1]

Willpower matters — and so do wages, cities, and food design.

Change one kitchen default this week: what is within arm’s reach at 9pm.

A fair society redesigns the aisle, not only the lecture.
