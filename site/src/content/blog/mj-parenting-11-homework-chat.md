Parent chats become second jobs and anxiety amplifiers.

WHO estimates that one in seven adolescents aged 10–19 experiences a mental disorder.[^1]

Your nervous system is a parenting tool.

Mute non-urgent school chats after 8pm.

Urgency is often just noise in a group thread.
