Scrolling past midnight borrows tomorrow’s patience.

CDC data for 2024 show 30.5% of U.S. adults average less than seven hours of sleep in a 24-hour period.[^1]

Grayscale the phone after 9pm for a week.

Night is for repair, not highlights.

End the false day.
