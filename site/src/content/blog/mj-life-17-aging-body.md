More years with disease unless defaults change.

WHO: adults living with diabetes rose from 200 million in 1990 to 830 million in 2022; more than half of adults aged 30+ with diabetes were not taking medication in 2022.[^1]

Strength work twice weekly — balance is freedom.

Independence has a muscle requirement.

Negotiate aging with training, not denial.
