Global obesity scale needs less shame and better defaults.

WHO reports that in 2022, one in eight people worldwide were living with obesity, and 2.5 billion adults were overweight.[^1]

Change one food default this week — not your entire personality.

Bodies keep the score of environments.

Kind structure beats cruel commentary.
