When housing outruns wages, “work harder” is a slogan.

OECD defines housing cost overburden as spending more than 40% of disposable income on housing — a threshold that hits many low-income renters across member countries.[^1]

Negotiate compensation with housing costs named out loud.

Participation trophies do not pay landlords.

Wage honesty includes the rent line.
