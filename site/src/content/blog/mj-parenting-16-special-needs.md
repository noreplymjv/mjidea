Care intensity rises when systems under-serve.

ILO analysis finds women perform about 76.2% of unpaid care work globally, with about 16.4 billion unpaid care hours every day.[^1]

Build a two-person relief plan — even two hours — on the calendar.

Stamina needs company.

Unnamed relief never arrives.
