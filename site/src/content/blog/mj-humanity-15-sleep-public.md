We brag about exhaustion like it proves worth.

CDC data for 2024 show 30.5% of U.S. adults average less than seven hours of sleep in a 24-hour period.[^1]

Sleep is not laziness. It is nervous-system maintenance.

Phone curfew 45 minutes before bed for seven nights.

A sleepless culture becomes cruel — first to itself.
