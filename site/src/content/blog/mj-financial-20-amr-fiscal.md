AMR already carries a massive associated death toll — costs follow.

WHO reports bacterial antimicrobial resistance was associated with more than 4.7 million deaths globally in 2021.[^1]

Stewardship in clinics and farms is economic policy.

Cheap antibiotics tomorrow depend on restraint today.

Future care budgets are being written in today’s prescriptions.
