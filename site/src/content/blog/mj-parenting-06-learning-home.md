When schools stumble, homes remain weather systems for language.

World Bank and partners estimated learning poverty in low- and middle-income countries near 70% after COVID disruptions — children unable to read and understand a simple text by age ten.[^1]

Ten pages aloud, three nights a week. Boring works.

A child who is read to learns they are worth time.

Shared reading is love with a spine.
