Heat used to mean summer. Now it means shift design and whether outdoor work is survivable.

WHO notes more than 2.4 billion workers are exposed to excessive heat globally, with more than 22.85 million occupational injuries each year linked to that exposure.[^1]

Heat is not only an environment story. It is a dignity story.

If you manage outdoor work, write water, shade, rest, and stop-work triggers before the next heatwave.

Shared atmosphere, shared duty.
