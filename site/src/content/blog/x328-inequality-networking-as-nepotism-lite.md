Shame is a terrible teacher and a thriving industry.

**networking as nepotism lite** thrives because it pays someone — a platform, a habit loop, a workplace norm — even when it costs you.

It shows up as irritability first — then as a story about how “this is just modern life.” A kitchen is full of food and somehow empty of a shared meal.

## The behavioral door

If status runs the show, put status on the healthier behavior.

## Make it easier to be decent

Do this: **open office hours public**. Keep the bar low enough that a tired human clears it. Raise the bar only after the ritual sticks.

Humanity’s younger half does not need more content about discipline. They need adults willing to change defaults in homes, schools, and products.

Keep the reframe. Drop the performance.
