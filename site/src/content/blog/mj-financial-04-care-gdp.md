Unpaid care is an economy hiding in plain sight.

ILO analysis finds women perform about 76.2% of unpaid care work globally, with about 16.4 billion unpaid care hours every day.[^1]

Count care leave as infrastructure in your organisation.

Price honesty begins by naming hours.

Invisible payrolls still exhaust people.
