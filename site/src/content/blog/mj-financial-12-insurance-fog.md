Chronic disease loads meet confusing coverage.

WHO: adults living with diabetes rose from 200 million in 1990 to 830 million in 2022; more than half of adults aged 30+ with diabetes were not taking medication in 2022.[^1]

Before signing, rewrite the claim path in five plain sentences — or walk.

If you cannot explain it, you do not have it.

Fog is not neutrality.
