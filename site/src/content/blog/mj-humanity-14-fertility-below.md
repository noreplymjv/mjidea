Low fertility is not a morality play. It is hostile math in housing and care.

UN World Population Prospects 2024: more than half of countries and areas have fertility below the replacement level of 2.1; global fertility is about 2.3, down from 3.3 around 1990.[^1]

People are not refusing life. Many are refusing impossible spreadsheets.

If you influence housing or work policy, ask whether a young family can survive the numbers.

Want more children? Make childhood less of a cliff.
