A kitchen is full of food and somehow empty of a shared meal.

Shame is a terrible teacher and a thriving industry. With **single use coffee cups**, the cost is rarely dramatic on day one. It is cumulative — like interest on a loan you did not mean to sign.

It shows up as irritability first — then as a story about how “this is just modern life.”

People reach for more motivation, more apps, more content. Rory-mode thinking says: change the choice architecture. If status runs the show, put status on the healthier behavior.

## The behavioral door

If status, ease, and default settings run the show, then “try harder” is a weak product. **keep-cup deposits** is a stronger one because it moves the easy path.

## Make it easier to be decent

Try it as a social object, not a private vow. Tell one person. Put it on a calendar. Remove one friction that makes the old habit win.

Younger people inherit systems older people built. Helping them means redesigning rooms, not blaming personalities.

Keep the reframe. Drop the performance.
