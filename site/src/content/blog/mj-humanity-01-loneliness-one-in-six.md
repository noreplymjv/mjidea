You can fill a city and still feel erasable. That feeling is not rare poetry.

The WHO Commission on Social Connection estimates about one in six people globally report loneliness, with loneliness associated with on the order of 871,000 deaths a year in the period examined.[^1]

Here is the flip: the opposite of loneliness is not a crowded calendar. It is being known — one reliable person beats forty weak ties.

Concrete move: schedule one real contact this week that cannot be replaced by an emoji. Protect it like a medical appointment.

Belonging is infrastructure. Societies that pretend otherwise keep paying in bodies.
