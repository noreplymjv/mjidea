Bodies grow inside engineered foodscapes.

WHO reports that in 2022, one in eight people worldwide were living with obesity, and 2.5 billion adults were overweight.[^1]

Structure changes intake better than sermons.

One family default swap — fruit visible, sugary drinks harder to reach.

Coach the environment, not only the child.
