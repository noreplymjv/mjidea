If the rational argument worked, we would already be fine.

**hallucinated facts** thrives because it pays someone — a platform, a habit loop, a workplace norm — even when it costs you.

Workers feel it on Sunday night, already bracing for a week that will not remember them. A young person can explain a crisis in the abstract and still not know who to text.

## Stop adding information

Swap a grand goal for a ridiculous small ritual that actually happens.

## A small redesign

Do this: **citation or silence**. Keep the bar low enough that a tired human clears it. Raise the bar only after the ritual sticks.

Humanity’s younger half does not need more content about discipline. They need adults willing to change defaults in homes, schools, and products.

The next generation does not need another sermon. They need a redesigned room.
