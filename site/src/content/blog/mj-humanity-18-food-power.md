The scandal is not that Earth cannot grow food.

UN agencies report around 733 million people faced hunger in 2023 — about one in eleven people globally.[^1]

“Food prices” often softens political failure and conflict.

Follow one serious food-security briefing a month — not only slogans.

Abundance without justice is a better-lit famine elsewhere.
