The spreadsheet is not wrong. It is just not what moves people.

**chore wars** thrives because it pays someone — a platform, a habit loop, a workplace norm — even when it costs you.

Younger people feel it as a low-grade panic that never quite becomes a sentence. A group sits together, each face lit from below, laughing at different jokes.

## What we keep getting wrong

Make the good option feel like taste, not virtue.

## What to do on Tuesday

Do this: **visible contribution charts**. Keep the bar low enough that a tired human clears it. Raise the bar only after the ritual sticks.

Humanity’s younger half does not need more content about discipline. They need adults willing to change defaults in homes, schools, and products.

If it only works when you feel inspired, it does not work.
