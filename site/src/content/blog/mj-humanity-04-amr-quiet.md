The terrifying future is a routine infection that shrugs at the pill.

WHO reports bacterial antimicrobial resistance was associated with more than 4.7 million deaths globally in 2021.[^1]

Every unnecessary antibiotic course is a withdrawal from a finite global account.

Never pressure a clinician for antibiotics “just in case.” Ask what is confirmed.

Civilisation can be only a working drug thick.
