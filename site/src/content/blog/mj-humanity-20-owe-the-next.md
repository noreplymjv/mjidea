Children do not vote on the defaults we normalize.

World Bank and partners estimated learning poverty in low- and middle-income countries near 70% after COVID disruptions — children unable to read and understand a simple text by age ten.[^1]

Legacy is not a brand film. It is what still works when you are gone.

Change one default that outlives your mood — a rule, a policy, a compounding gift.

Live like the next readers are already watching.
