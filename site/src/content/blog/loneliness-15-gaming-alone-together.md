Call **gaming alone together** what it is: a design outcome wearing a moral costume.

Nobody wakes up craving another lecture. They wake up inside a system. Neighbors feel it when they stop knowing the names of the people who share their air.

A policy PDF exists. A habit still wins.

## A different game board

Design for the tired version of yourself, not the TED-talk version. That sounds soft until you watch how fast a small ritual beats a grand resolution.

The practical door is **local LAN / club nights**. Not forever. For the next fourteen days. Then review like an adult.

## Practice, not posture

Society loves stories of sudden transformation. Culture actually moves when the better option becomes ordinary.

If this touches you, it should also slightly annoy the part of you that wants a dramatic rebirth. Good. Annoyance means the redesign is real.

Ask what the tired version of you will still do. Build for that person.
