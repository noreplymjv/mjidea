Shelter that consumes your future is not safety.

OECD defines housing cost overburden as spending more than 40% of disposable income on housing — a threshold that hits many low-income renters across member countries.[^1]

If you are in overburden territory, treat lifestyle upgrades as threats.

Calculate housing costs over disposable income this week — honestly.

Money stress is often housing stress in nicer clothes.
