Call **solo holidays** what it is: a design outcome wearing a moral costume.

We treat this like a knowledge gap. It is often a status gap. You can be informed about everything and intimate with almost nothing.

The “quick check” lasts forty minutes and leaves a sticky aftertaste.

## Stop adding information

Solve for belonging first; the rest often follows. That sounds soft until you watch how fast a small ritual beats a grand resolution.

The practical door is **shared volunteer days**. Not forever. For the next fourteen days. Then review like an adult.

## A small redesign

Society loves stories of sudden transformation. Culture actually moves when the better option becomes ordinary.

If this touches you, it should also slightly annoy the part of you that wants a dramatic rebirth. Good. Annoyance means the redesign is real.

Make the good option the path of least resistance — then protect it.
