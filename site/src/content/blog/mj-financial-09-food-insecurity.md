Hunger staying high for years is a stalled moral project.

UN agencies report around 733 million people faced hunger in 2023 — about one in eleven people globally.[^1]

Household buffer of basics; support food banks that publish impact.

The trolley tells the truth faster than speeches.

Secure food is secure politics.
