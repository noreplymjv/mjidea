Food insecurity is how inflation feels at the bottom.

UN agencies report around 733 million people faced hunger in 2023 — about one in eleven people globally.[^1]

Track your real basket monthly, not only headlines.

Policy that ignores the trolley misses the voter.

Lived prices are the real index.
