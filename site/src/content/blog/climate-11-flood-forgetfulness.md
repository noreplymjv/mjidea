Call **flood forgetfulness** what it is: a design outcome wearing a moral costume.

We keep buying tools for a problem that is actually a default. You feel it in the evening, when the day is gone and you cannot name what you did with your mind.

Someone unlocks a phone before their eyes have fully opened.

## The reframe

Do not add information. Change the social reward. That sounds soft until you watch how fast a small ritual beats a grand resolution.

The practical door is **memory markers in towns**. Not forever. For the next fourteen days. Then review like an adult.

## A door that opens

Society loves stories of sudden transformation. Culture actually moves when the better option becomes ordinary.

If this touches you, it should also slightly annoy the part of you that wants a dramatic rebirth. Good. Annoyance means the redesign is real.

Start smaller than your pride wants. That is usually where the door actually is.
