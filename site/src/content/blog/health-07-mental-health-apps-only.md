Call **mental health apps only** what it is: a design outcome wearing a moral costume.

## What we keep getting wrong

Replace inspiration with a calendar invite. That sounds soft until you watch how fast a small ritual beats a grand resolution.

The practical door is **body + people**. Not forever. For the next fourteen days. Then review like an adult.

## What to do on Tuesday

If this touches you, it should also slightly annoy the part of you that wants a dramatic rebirth. Good. Annoyance means the redesign is real.
