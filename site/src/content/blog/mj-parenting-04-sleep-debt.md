Parenting on fumes is not a virtue badge.

CDC data for 2024 show 30.5% of U.S. adults average less than seven hours of sleep in a 24-hour period.[^1]

Rested love hears better than exhausted love.

Trade one evening obligation for an earlier night this week.

Tired love is still love — rested love is clearer.
