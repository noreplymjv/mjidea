Heavy social use and sleep problems travel together in the evidence base adults ignore.

The 2023 U.S. Surgeon General advisory notes social media use is nearly universal among U.S. teens (up to 95% of ages 13–17), and research it cites links more than three hours a day to roughly double the risk of certain poor mental-health outcomes.[^1]

Buy alarm clocks if you must — reclaim the night.

Tomorrow’s mood is last night’s screen.

Protect sleep like you protect seatbelts.
