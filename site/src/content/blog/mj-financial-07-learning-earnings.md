Learning poverty threatens lifetime earnings at civilisation scale.

World Bank and partners estimated learning poverty in low- and middle-income countries near 70% after COVID disruptions — children unable to read and understand a simple text by age ten.[^1]

Back tutoring like infrastructure.

Enrollment without reading is a hollow statistic.

Underpowered readers become underpowered citizens.
