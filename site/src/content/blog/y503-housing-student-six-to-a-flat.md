Call **student six to a flat** what it is: a design outcome wearing a moral costume.

Younger people did not invent this mess. They inherited the interface. Parents feel it when they realize the house is full of screens and empty of unhurried talk.

A calendar is packed, yet the week feels strangely uninhabited.

## The behavioral door

Reduce friction for the right act; add a little sand to the wrong one. That sounds soft until you watch how fast a small ritual beats a grand resolution.

The practical door is **enforcement + supply**. Not forever. For the next fourteen days. Then review like an adult.

## Make it easier to be decent

Society loves stories of sudden transformation. Culture actually moves when the better option becomes ordinary.

If this touches you, it should also slightly annoy the part of you that wants a dramatic rebirth. Good. Annoyance means the redesign is real.

Put the better behavior where status already lives — or move status.
