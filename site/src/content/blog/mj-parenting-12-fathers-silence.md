Stigma still teaches boys to vanish inside.

WHO estimates that one in seven adolescents aged 10–19 experiences a mental disorder.[^1]

Name one feeling out loud this week without fixing it.

Emotional fluency is a gift you model, not a lecture.

Silence trains the next silence.
