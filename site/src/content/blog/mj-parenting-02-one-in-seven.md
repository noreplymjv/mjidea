We minimize teen storms until they become adult scars.

WHO estimates that one in seven adolescents aged 10–19 experiences a mental disorder.[^1]

Ask “what hurts?” before “why aren’t you trying?”

Keep a clinician shortlist before crisis. Normalise help like dentistry.

A silent teen is sometimes rationing words.
