Your future self is built while you are unconscious.

CDC data for 2024 show 30.5% of U.S. adults average less than seven hours of sleep in a 24-hour period.[^1]

Same wake time for seven days; let bedtime follow.

Guard the night like training.

Rest is not optional equipment.
