Not every parenting tip survives hunger.

UNICEF and the World Bank estimate 333 million children — about one in six — live in extreme poverty on less than US$2.15 a day.[^1]

Child poverty is adult policy wearing small shoes.

When you vote or donate, ask what reaches children — food, school, cash to caregivers.

Morality is measurable at the bottom of a child’s shopping list.
