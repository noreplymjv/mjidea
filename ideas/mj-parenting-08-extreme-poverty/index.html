When childhood is under $2.15

Not every tip survives hunger.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · parenting · parenting · poverty

← All ideas

Advice culture often assumes a full fridge.

UNICEF and the World Bank estimate 333 million children — about one in six — live in extreme poverty on less than US$2.15 a day.1

A full lunchbox is pedagogy.

Fund child-focused basics when you can; fight meal stigma when you teach.

Dignity starts with calories and respect.
