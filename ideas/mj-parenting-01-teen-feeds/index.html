The feed is raising someone

Custody of attention is the real fight.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · parenting · parenting · tech

← All ideas

Your child has two rooms: the bedroom and the infinite scroll.

The 2023 U.S. Surgeon General advisory notes social media use is nearly universal among U.S. teens (up to 95% of ages 13–17), and research it cites links more than three hours a day to roughly double the risk of certain poor mental-health outcomes.1

You are not behind on tech. You are early on responsibility.

Co-create a family tech contract — hours, bedrooms, consequences — and follow it yourself.

Children copy what adults protect.
