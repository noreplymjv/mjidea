Precarity plus temperature

Flexibility without protection is risk transfer.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · financial · gig · heat

← All ideas

Precarious outdoor work meets a hotter planet.

WHO notes more than 2.4 billion workers are exposed to excessive heat globally, with more than 22.85 million occupational injuries each year linked to that exposure.1

If you hire gig labour, write heat and water into the contract.

Risk transfer is not innovation.

Contracts should outlive weather apps.
