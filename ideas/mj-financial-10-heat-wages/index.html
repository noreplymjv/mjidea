Heat taxes labour’s body

Dead workers do not ship orders.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · financial · heat · labour

← All ideas

Hot work without protocol is deferred injury.

WHO notes more than 2.4 billion workers are exposed to excessive heat globally, with more than 22.85 million occupational injuries each year linked to that exposure.1

Price rest breaks as safety in hot trades.

Water, shade, stop-work — write them down.

Productivity fantasies die in heatstroke.
