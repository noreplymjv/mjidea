The oldest prescription

Sidewalks are clinics.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · life · walking · life

← All ideas

Walking undercuts inactivity one step at a time.

WHO reports nearly 1.8 billion adults — about 31% — did not meet recommended physical activity levels in 2022.1

Walk the first call of the day.

Sidewalks are clinics if you use them.

Oldest prescription, still underfilled.
