The missing third adult

Networks need tending.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · parenting · parenting · family

← All ideas

Loneliness hits elders and new parents alike.

The WHO Commission on Social Connection estimates about one in six people globally report loneliness, with loneliness associated with on the order of 871,000 deaths a year in the period examined.1

Schedule one intergenerational ritual — call or visit — that is not residual.

Families are networks. Networks need tending.

The third adult is often the difference between drowning and swimming.
