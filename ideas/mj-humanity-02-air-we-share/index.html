The air we pretend is free

Dirty air is a quiet tax on shared lungs.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · humanity · air · health

← All ideas

Breathing is the one subscription you cannot cancel.

WHO estimates the combined effects of ambient and household air pollution are associated with about seven million premature deaths each year.1

Pollution feels invisible until a child wheezes. Make the invisible countable.

Track AQI on your commute for a week. Change time or route when it spikes — then ask what the local source is.

A species that will not defend its shared lung will keep calling the damage weather.
