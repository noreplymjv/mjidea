830 million and half untreated

The quiet climb in blood sugar.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · humanity · diabetes · health

← All ideas

Some emergencies scream. This one whispers through glucose.

WHO: adults living with diabetes rose from 200 million in 1990 to 830 million in 2022; more than half of adults aged 30+ with diabetes were not taking medication in 2022.1

Detection, stigma, and access decide who gets medicine.

If you are due for screening, book it. If you manage a workplace, make screening easy.

The future arrives as a lab number before it arrives as a headline.
