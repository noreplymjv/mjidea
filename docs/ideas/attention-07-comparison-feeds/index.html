The human fix for comparison feeds

Comparison feeds is a human problem with a workable door: unfollow envy sources for 30 days.

7 July 2026 · attention · attention · humanity · solutions · youth

← All ideas

Call comparison feeds what it is: a design outcome wearing a moral costume.

The market sold convenience. Character paid the invoice. The body keeps the score even when the feed resets the mood.

Two people argue about values while both refreshing a feed that profits from the argument.

What we keep getting wrong

Replace inspiration with a calendar invite. That sounds soft until you watch how fast a small ritual beats a grand resolution.

The practical door is unfollow envy sources for 30 days. Not forever. For the next fourteen days. Then review like an adult.

What to do on Tuesday

Society loves stories of sudden transformation. Culture actually moves when the better option becomes ordinary.

If this touches you, it should also slightly annoy the part of you that wants a dramatic rebirth. Good. Annoyance means the redesign is real.

Do the boring version. Boring is how culture actually changes.
