Talent lost to care is fiction.

Care responsibilities keep hundreds of millions of women outside paid work.

ILO estimates that in 2023, 708 million women cited care responsibilities as the reason for being outside the labour market.1

Measure promotions by caregiving status.

Retention requires care design.

Sidelined talent is a national loss.
