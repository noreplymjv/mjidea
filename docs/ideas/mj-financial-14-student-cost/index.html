Education priced like luxury

Who gets to think for a living?

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · financial · education · cost

← All ideas

We can underfund reading and overcharge credentials at once.

World Bank and partners estimated learning poverty in low- and middle-income countries near 70% after COVID disruptions — children unable to read and understand a simple text by age ten.1

Prefer employers who fund learning over those who only demand degrees.

Price signals shape minds.

Access is the real entrance exam.
