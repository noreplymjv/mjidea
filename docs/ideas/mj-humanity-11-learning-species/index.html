When ten-year-olds cannot read

Enrollment without learning is ceremony.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · humanity · education · children

← All ideas

A child who cannot read a simple text by age ten is locked out of the map.

World Bank and partners estimated learning poverty in low- and middle-income countries near 70% after COVID disruptions — children unable to read and understand a simple text by age ten.1

Learning is the point — not the attendance sheet.

One hour of shared reading aloud a week with a child — no screens.

Species that cannot teach their young to read will not stay free long.
