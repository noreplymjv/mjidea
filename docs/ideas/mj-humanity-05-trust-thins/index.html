Trust thins before systems crack

Printed trust is not grown trust.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · humanity · trust · institutions

← All ideas

Trust is the quiet permission that lets strangers cooperate.

The WHO Commission on Social Connection estimates about one in six people globally report loneliness, with loneliness associated with on the order of 871,000 deaths a year in the period examined.1

Pair institutional fog with loneliness at population scale and you see a double fracture.

Wherever you lead, publish one falsifiable claim this month — a number people can check.

Grown trust beats printed trust.
