Attention is a ballot

Thumbs cast votes all day.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · life · attention · life

← All ideas

What you look at becomes who you rehearse being.

The 2023 U.S. Surgeon General advisory notes social media use is nearly universal among U.S. teens (up to 95% of ages 13–17), and research it cites links more than three hours a day to roughly double the risk of certain poor mental-health outcomes.1

Remove one app from the home screen for a week.

Your thumbs are casting votes.

Attention is character practice.
