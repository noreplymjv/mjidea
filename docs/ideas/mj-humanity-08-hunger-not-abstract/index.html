733 million is not a chart

Hunger should end dinner-table abstraction.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · humanity · hunger · food

← All ideas

Hunger is the oldest injustice wearing new supply chains.

UN agencies report around 733 million people faced hunger in 2023 — about one in eleven people globally.1

Food abundance in one postcode and undernourishment in another is a design of power and price.

If you give, fund agencies that measure outcomes; if you cook, waste less as practice, not trend.

A full fridge proves your map is local — not that the world is fine.
