The third guest at the table

Presence is a dish you serve.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · parenting · parenting · attention

← All ideas

A phone at dinner teaches children they are interruptible by strangers.

The 2023 U.S. Surgeon General advisory notes social media use is nearly universal among U.S. teens (up to 95% of ages 13–17), and research it cites links more than three hours a day to roughly double the risk of certain poor mental-health outcomes.1

Family meals are one of the last offline commons.

Phone basket for twenty minutes of dinner — adults included.

What you look at at the table becomes what they think matters.
