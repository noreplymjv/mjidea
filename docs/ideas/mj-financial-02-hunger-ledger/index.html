Hunger is a balance sheet

733 million is an indictment.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · financial · hunger · money

← All ideas

Hunger is how economics fails in the stomach.

UN agencies report around 733 million people faced hunger in 2023 — about one in eleven people globally.1

Ask how policy moves food prices for the poorest.

If you invest or vote, follow food-security outcomes, not slogans.

Markets that cannot feed people eventually eat trust.
