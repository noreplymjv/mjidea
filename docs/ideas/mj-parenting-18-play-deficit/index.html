Play is not a luxury add-on

Play metabolises the world.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · parenting · parenting · play

← All ideas

Overloaded kids need play as much as worksheets need margins.

WHO estimates that one in seven adolescents aged 10–19 experiences a mental disorder.1

Protect one hour of unstructured play weekly — adult phones away.

Play is how children metabolise the world.

A childhood without play is a rushed adult in training.
