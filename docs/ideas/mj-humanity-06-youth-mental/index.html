One in seven adolescents

Youth distress is not a phase to wait out.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · humanity · youth · mental

← All ideas

We keep treating adolescent pain like weather that will pass on its own.

WHO estimates that one in seven adolescents aged 10–19 experiences a mental disorder.1

Youth distress is phones and sleep and belonging and whether help feels normal.

If you parent, teach, or coach teens — one honest check-in a week that is not about grades.

The adults we become are partly the adolescents we refused to see.
