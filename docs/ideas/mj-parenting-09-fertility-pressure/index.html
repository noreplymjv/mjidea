The spreadsheet that blocks the crib

Ideology is not childcare.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · parenting · parenting · fertility

← All ideas

“Just have kids” without housing and care is ideology.

UN World Population Prospects 2024: more than half of countries and areas have fertility below the replacement level of 2.1; global fertility is about 2.3, down from 3.3 around 1990.1

Talk money and care hours honestly with young adults you advise.

Want more children in a society? Make childhood less of a financial cliff.

Demography follows whether life is livable.
