When teaching to the test becomes the air you breathe

Teaching to the test is a human problem with a workable door: portfolio evidence of skill.

27 September 2026 · education · education · humanity · solutions · youth

← All ideas

We keep buying tools for a problem that is actually a default. You feel it in the evening, when the day is gone and you cannot name what you did with your mind.

The problem shows up as teaching to the test — ordinary, almost respectable, until you notice what it quietly spends.

Someone unlocks a phone before their eyes have fully opened. We keep treating this like a personal failure of will. Willpower is a thin currency. Environments print their own money.

The reframe

Here is the flip that matters: Do not add information. Change the social reward. Information rarely loses to laziness. It loses to whatever feels easier, safer, or higher-status in the moment.

A door that opens

A workable move is not a slogan. It is portfolio evidence of skill. Make it visible. Make it slightly inconvenient to skip. Make it feel like taste when you keep it.

For younger generations especially, lectures about character bounce off interfaces built to harvest impulse. If you want different people, change what the room rewards — then practice the new ritual until it is boring.

None of this requires you to become a monk. It asks you to stop romanticizing heroics and start redesigning defaults. Start smaller than your pride wants. That is usually where the door actually is.
