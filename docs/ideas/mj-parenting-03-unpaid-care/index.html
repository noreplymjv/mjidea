The second shift has a name

Love does not make hours free.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · parenting · parenting · care

← All ideas

Unpaid care is the invisible second job of family life.

ILO analysis finds women perform about 76.2% of unpaid care work globally, with about 16.4 billion unpaid care hours every day.1

Unnamed work stays gendered by default.

Put care tasks on a shared list with names and times.

Children learn gender from who clears the plate.
