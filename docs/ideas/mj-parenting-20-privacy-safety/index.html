Trust with a latch

Control without trust becomes secrecy.

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · parenting · parenting · trust

← All ideas

Teens need privacy; parents need enough access to keep them alive.

The 2023 U.S. Surgeon General advisory notes social media use is nearly universal among U.S. teens (up to 95% of ages 13–17), and research it cites links more than three hours a day to roughly double the risk of certain poor mental-health outcomes.1

Shared locations for late nights; private journals untouched.

Control without trust becomes secrecy. Trust without a latch becomes neglect.

The latch is love with boundaries.
