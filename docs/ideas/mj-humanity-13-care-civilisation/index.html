16.4 billion unpaid hours a day

Care is infrastructure, not “help.”

Mayank Jain · 27 August 2026 · humanity · care · gender

← All ideas

Someone is holding the baby and the elder while spreadsheets call them inactive.

ILO analysis finds women perform about 76.2% of unpaid care work globally, with about 16.4 billion unpaid care hours every day.1

Care is civilisation’s hidden payroll.

Name the care load in your household in hours this week. Redistribute one block.

Economies that erase care will keep miscounting what keeps them alive.
