The workable door on AI tutors replacing struggle

AI tutors replacing struggle is a human problem with a workable door: desirable difficulty.

23 August 2026 · education · education · humanity · solutions

← All ideas

Most advice on this starts too late.

AI tutors replacing struggle thrives because it pays someone — a platform, a habit loop, a workplace norm — even when it costs you.

The weird part is how normal it looks from the outside. A good intention dies in the gap between the app icon and the door.

A different game board

Stop asking how to motivate people. Ask what the default makes easy.

Practice, not posture

Do this: desirable difficulty. Keep the bar low enough that a tired human clears it. Raise the bar only after the ritual sticks.

Humanity’s younger half does not need more content about discipline. They need adults willing to change defaults in homes, schools, and products.

Write the ritual down. Rituals beat moods.
